Last week, during a brutal heatwave, we had the great pleasure to have a new PhD graduation from our lab!
Hien defended her thesis from the questions of her two examiners,
[Caroline Wendling](https://www.mvp.uni-muenchen.de/microbiology/wendling/) and
[Marius Vital](https://www.mhh.de/en/institute-for-medical-mikcobiology-and-hospital-epidemiology/work-group-vital).
Hien did a great job, and has a result she did not need to make any changes to her dissertation.
